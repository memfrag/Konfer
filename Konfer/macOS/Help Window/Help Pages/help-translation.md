# Translation

Any finished transcript can be read, and exported, in one of Konfer's other languages. macOS's own translation does the work, so this also stays on your Mac.

## Translating a meeting

1. Open the meeting and choose **Translate…** in the bar above the transcript.
2. Pick the language under **Into:** and choose **Translate**.

An hour of meeting takes about two minutes. Lines appear as they are translated, and until a line has been translated it shows, dimmed, as it was said. You can keep reading, and stop at any point from the bottom of the sidebar; the lines already done are kept.

Once there is a translation, the bar switches between the original and the translation. A meeting holds one translation at a time. The **…** menu in the bar has **Translate Into…** to replace it, and **Remove Translation** to discard it. The original transcript is never changed.

## Language packs

The first time you translate between two languages, macOS may need to download them. The Translate sheet says so, and **Download…** brings up macOS's own download prompt. These packs belong to macOS rather than Konfer, so they aren't among Konfer's models. You manage them in **System Settings ▸ General ▸ Language & Region ▸ Translation Languages**.

## Pairs macOS can't translate

macOS translates nearly every pair of Konfer's ten languages, but not **Swedish ↔ Polish** or **Danish ↔ Polish**. The Translate sheet marks them **not available**. Konfer doesn't work around this by going through English. You would get a fluent sentence that is a translation of a translation, and nothing on screen would tell you so.

## After editing

Editing a line, or splitting or merging lines, discards the affected translations, because they no longer match what was said. The bar then counts the lines that aren't translated, and **Translate the Rest** translates only those, which takes a fraction of a second each. **Transcribe Again…** discards the whole translation.

Translated subtitles use the same timings as the original ones. See [Exporting](konfer-help:exporting).
