# Recording a Meeting

Choose **File ▸ New Recording…** (⇧⌘R), or **Record a Meeting…** from the **+** menu in the sidebar.

<view tag="open-window" window="recorder" label="Open the Recorder"/>

## What to record

- **Microphone.** The voices in the room, including yours. Choose **Off — the other side only** when you are only listening to a call. Then nobody in the room is recorded, and macOS won't ask for microphone access.
- **Also record.** The other side of a call:
  - **Nothing — microphone only** is for a meeting that happens entirely in the room.
  - **An app**, such as Zoom, Teams or a browser, records only that app, so notifications and music stay out. The list shows the apps currently playing sound, so start the call first. macOS asks for permission to record system audio the first time.
  - **Everything the Mac plays** records every sound, notifications included. macOS asks for the full screen-recording permission the first time.

Konfer keeps the two sides apart: the microphone on the left channel and the call on the right. That is how the transcript can tell someone in the room from someone on the call, even when two voices sound alike. A recording takes about 350 MB an hour.

**Save to** and **Name** decide where the file goes. Konfer suggests your Downloads folder and remembers any other folder you choose. Press **Record**, or Return, to start, and **Stop** to finish. The two meters show what each side is hearing.

## Warnings while you record

**Nothing is coming from the other side.** The call's side has been silent for several seconds. When macOS refuses system-audio permission, it doesn't say so; it just hands Konfer silence. Check **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Screen & System Audio Recording**, and check that the call is actually playing. **Open Privacy Settings…** takes you there.

**The microphone is picking up the call.** The call is coming out of your speakers and into the microphone. Headphones fix this. If you can't use them, keep recording, and turn on **The call came out of the speakers** when you transcribe. Konfer will then ignore the microphone wherever it only hears the call. Otherwise, people on the call can end up listed as being in the room.

## When you stop

The Transcribe sheet opens for the new recording. See [Transcribing a File](konfer-help:transcribing). If you cancel it, the recording stays where you saved it, and you can drop it onto Konfer later.
