# People

When you name a speaker in a meeting, Konfer remembers what their voice sounds like. The next time it hears someone similar, it asks, beside the speaker at the top of the meeting:

> Sounds like Anna?

Click it to accept, and the speaker is named. Ignore it, and nothing happens. Konfer never names anyone on its own. A wrong guess would put a real person's name on an hour of someone else's words, and "Speaker 2" is the far better mistake.

Every time you name or accept someone, Konfer gets a better idea of their voice.

## The People window

**Window ▸ People**, or the people button above the sidebar, lists everyone Konfer knows. For each person it shows how many recordings they were heard in, and when they were first and last heard.

<view tag="open-window" window="people" label="Open People"/>

- **Rename…** changes the name Konfer suggests.
- **Description** is a note about who they are and how you know them. It's shown here, and when you point at a suggestion.
- **Same Person As** merges two entries that turned out to be one person.
- **Delete** forgets a voice. Meetings that already carry the name keep it.

Voices are stored on your Mac, like everything else in Konfer.
